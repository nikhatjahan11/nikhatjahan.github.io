How I Work

I own the full design cycle

From needs analysis and learning objectives through storyboarding, Storyline or Rise build, and post launch iteration. I drive the entire process, not just the production stage.

I design consequences, not just content

My branching scenarios put learners inside real decisions with realistic outcomes. The goal is to make the cost of poor choices visible before they happen in the real world.

I've worked directly with SMEs

At PhysicsWallah, Vedantu and Leap Scholar, I've collaborated closely with subject experts to validate content, keeping courses accurate without slowing down production.

I treat failure as design data

I piloted a young learner course, watched it underperform, redesigned it from the ground up using what I learned, and now lead that revived programme as Head of Department.

I'm built for startup environments

Fast moving edtech is all I've known. I take ownership, manage ambiguity, and deliver without needing a rigid structure around me. Available remotely or otherwise.

I design for very different learners

Young children, school students, and adult professionals each require an entirely different design approach. I've built courses for all three and know where those differences actually matter.

Articulate Storyline 360 · Scenario Based Learning · Corporate

How to Give Feedback When It Really Matters

Even strong managers who excel at performance metrics often struggle with interpersonal feedback. After seven years across education and corporate contexts, one pattern kept appearing: the gap is not knowledge. It is the moment of delivery. Feedback gets given too harshly, too casually, or not at all. This course was built to put learners inside that moment.

Learners meet Priya, a team member missing deadlines, and navigate three branching choices with real consequences before committing. A second scene reveals what happens when Priya is heard versus reprimanded, making the human cost of poor feedback visible rather than theoretical.

Sole instructional designer. Responsibilities included needs identification, storyboarding, and full Storyline build. Published as a live web based course. SCORM 1.2 package built and tested; available on request for LMS deployment.
Learners leave able to
Apply a structured approach to delivering feedback in real workplace situations, evaluate the consequences of different feedback styles before acting, and reflect on the human impact of how feedback is delivered.

Rise 360 · Young Learners · Self Initiated

Podcast Basics for Young Creators

Podcasting is one of the fastest growing creative formats today, yet most 10 to 14 year olds encounter it with no structured foundation. The format is everywhere, but the knowledge of how it works, what types exist, and how to begin is not being taught. This course was built to close that gap at exactly the right age.

Three lessons take complete beginners from zero assumed knowledge through what a podcast is, exploring types via embedded audio and video, to hands on practice through MCQs, flashcards, and drag and drop activities. Every concept is introduced before it is tested.

Sole instructional designer. Responsibilities included curriculum design, content sequencing, and full Rise 360 build.
Learners leave able to
Identify what a podcast is and how it differs from other audio formats, distinguish between different podcast types using real examples, and independently begin planning their own podcast.
